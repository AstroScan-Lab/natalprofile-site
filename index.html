      02Not a sun-sign horoscope

      A sun-sign horoscope is one point out of dozens

      “You're a Taurus” only means the Sun was in Taurus on the day you were born. The same is true for one twelfth of humanity.

      Sun-sign horoscope

      • Looks only at the Sun.
      • One text for everyone born in the same month.
      • Knows neither your birth time nor your birthplace.

      A birth chart reading

      • Sun, Moon and planets, twelve houses and the links between them.
      • Calculated for your date, time and place of birth.
      • The text is written from your calculations and explains them in everyday words.

      HOW OTHERS SEE YOU

      At a first meeting you come across as a very confident, bright and warm person. People see you as open and generous, someone who easily ends up at the centre of attention and seems to give off light. It looks as if nothing frightens you and you are happy to share your good mood with everyone around.

      WHO YOU REALLY ARE

      Behind that bright cover is a person who values calm and reliability above all. The outward confidence is more of a calling card, a way of presenting yourself to the world. In truth you do not need constant attention. What matters far more is solid ground under your feet: a cosy home, a steady income, a predictable tomorrow and a small circle of loyal friends.

      WHERE YOUR STRENGTH LIES

      Your rare quality is a mix of great responsibility and unconventional thinking. You can carry demanding tasks for years, the kind that take real discipline, and still find completely unexpected, fresh solutions where others have hit a dead end. At work, for example, you can not only get every routine matter running smoothly but also suggest an entirely new way of doing it that makes life easier for everyone.

      08Questions

      Frequently asked questions

      I don't know my time of birth. What should I do?

      Choose a report “without birth time”. It leaves out houses and the ascendant, but character, feelings, thinking and relationships are described without them.

      How is this different from a zodiac-sign horoscope?

      A sign horoscope uses only the position of the Sun. A report calculates all planets, houses and the links between them for your date, time and place of birth.

      Who writes the text of the report?

      Planet positions are calculated by software using the Swiss Ephemeris. A language model then writes the text from those calculations under fixed rules.

      Which languages are reports available in?

      Fifteen: English, Russian, Spanish, Hindi, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Turkish, Indonesian, Uzbek, Kazakh, French, German, Italian, Arabic and Persian.

      Can I get a refund?

      A report is created for you personally, so payment is not refunded after the PDF is delivered. If a report was not generated through our fault, a full refund is issued.
