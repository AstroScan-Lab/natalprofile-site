About the project

What it is

NatalProfile is a Telegram bot that turns birth data into a personal PDF report. It covers three fields: Western astrology, the Vedic chart (Jyotish) and numerology.

How reports are calculated

  • Planet positions, houses and aspects are calculated by software using the Swiss Ephemeris.
  • The Vedic chart uses the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsha and Whole Sign houses.
  • Numerology follows the Pythagorean system: the digits of the date and the letters of the name.
  • The text is written by a language model strictly from the finished calculations. In the yearly and daily forecasts, dates and periods are set by the software, not by the model.

What we do not do

  • We do not predict events: forecasts give themes and periods, not “what will happen”.
  • We do not give medical, financial or legal advice.
  • We do not sell a subscription: each report is paid for separately.
