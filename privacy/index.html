Privacy Policy NatalProfile

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NatalProfile ("we", "our bot") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when using our bot on Telegram.

1. Information We Collect

To generate a personal astrological report, we request:

  • Your name (or nickname);
  • Gender;
  • Date, time, and place of birth;
  • Partner's name, gender, date, time, place of birth (for compatibility);
  • Child's name, gender, date, time, place of birth (for child chart).
  • Date, time and city of birth — when you calculate a chart on natalprofile.com: they are sent to our server for the calculation only and are not stored.

We also technically receive your Telegram user ID to ensure bot functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely for:

  • Calculating astrological parameters (natal chart, cosmogram, compatibility);
  • Generating a personal PDF report;
  • Sending the completed report to you in chat;
  • Improving bot performance (on an anonymized basis).

We do not use your data for any other purpose, including advertising mailings.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Your name, gender, and date, time and place of birth are deleted from our database as soon as the report has been generated and sent to you.
  • If an order is not completed (the form is not finished or the payment does not go through) or the report cannot be generated, this data is deleted automatically within 7 days at the latest.
  • The finished PDF file is kept on the server for 24 hours and then deleted. So that you can open your chart again in the mini app, we keep only the calculated planetary positions — without the name, date, time or place of birth.
  • Anonymized statistics may be used for analysis and improvement.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or exchange your personal data and do not share it for advertising. To fulfil your order, we use services that process data only for that purpose:

  • Google (Gemini API) — writes the text of the report. It receives the name, gender and the calculated chart indicators (such as planetary positions and aspects); for numerology — the name and date of birth. The time and place of birth are not sent.
  • Open-Meteo — suggests the city as you type. It receives only the city name you entered.
  • Google Sheets — sales records: order number, Telegram ID, product, language and date, without names or birth data.
  • Telegram — chatting with the bot, delivering the report and payment in Stars.

Beyond this, data is disclosed only where required by law (e.g., upon request from authorized government bodies).

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, internet transmission is not completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about stored data;
  • Request deletion of your data before report generation;
  • Withdraw consent by ceasing bot use.

Contact us at @NatalProfileSupport to exercise these rights.

7. Consent

By continuing to use NatalProfile, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and consent to data collection and processing as described.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this link: https://t.me/natalprofile_bot.
